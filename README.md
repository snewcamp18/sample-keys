# Sample Keys

Turn your own recordings into a playable instrument. Drop sounds onto a keyboard, shape them in a waveform
editor, play them from a MIDI controller or the screen, and sequence patterns.

## Use it

Open the app in a browser. To keep it like an app that works offline:

- **iPhone / iPad:** open it in Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open it in Chrome, tap **⋮ → Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
- **Mac / Windows:** open it in Chrome or Edge and click the install icon in the address bar.

Sign in with **SYNC** (Google or email) to keep your instruments in step across your devices. Without signing in,
everything is saved on the device you're using.

## Features

- Map one sound across the whole keyboard (pitched) or one sound per key (kit mode)
- WAV, AIFF, MP3, M4A (including iPhone voice memos), OGG and FLAC
- Waveform editor: start/end trim, loop points, fades, gain, tuning, reverse, normalize, silence trimming
- Attack, release, tone, reverb, velocity and pitch-bend controls
- MIDI controllers in Chrome and Edge on Mac, Windows and Android (iPhone browsers don't support MIDI)
- Piano-roll step sequencer with swing, live recording and a click track
- Soft and loud velocity layers: record each key twice and the app picks the take that matches how hard you play
- Record straight from the microphone, then keep it as one sound or auto-map it into notes
- Auto-map: split one recording of many notes (e.g. every piano key in order) into one sound per key
- Share an instrument with a link; family and friends add their own copy
- Big uncompressed recordings (e.g. 32-bit float WAV from a field recorder) are compressed to AAC automatically, about 18× smaller
- Export and import instruments as `.json` files

Your instruments are private to your account. Setting up your own copy: see [SETUP.md](SETUP.md).
